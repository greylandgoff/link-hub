Current Website Analysis:
	•	Content Layout
	•	What works: clear headline, single “Book Now” button above the fold, concise bio.
	•	What falls short: key benefits (“discreet companion,” “LGBTQ-friendly”) live deep down the page; walls of text on mobile slow scanning; inconsistent heading styles hide structure.
	•	Interactivity
	•	Current: hover highlights on links; basic contact form.
	•	Missing: no calendar picker, no quick-reply chat option, no progress feedback when the form submits (so people wonder if it worked).
	•	Multimedia Use
	•	Present: static hero photo and a small gallery of snaps.
	•	Opportunity: short muted autoplay video in hero; client-quote overlay on images; subtle scroll animations to signal new sections.

Create two User Personas:
	1.	Jake

	•	Pain Points: unsure if service matches hype; form feels clunky on phone.
	2.	Marcus
	•	Age 45 · Male · Local Entrepreneur
	•	Goals: book discreetly for networking dinners; repeat the experience easily next month.

Provide UX Enhancement Recommendations:
	•	Content Layout
	•	Move key selling points into a three-column “Why Book” block just under the hero.
	•	Standardize H2 labels (“About,” “How It Works,” “Testimonials,” “Contact”) so scanners instantly know where they are.
	•	Swap dense paragraphs for bullet lists of benefits and steps.
	•	Interactivity
	•	Embed a date-and-time picker (e.g., Calendly) directly in the page to cut form steps in half.
	•	Add a sticky mini-bar or floating “Book Now” that stays visible on scroll.
	•	Introduce a live-chat widget with canned quick-reply options (“Available tonight?,” “Rates?”).
	•	Multimedia
	•	Create a 20-second hero video on mute (tap-to-unmute) introducing Bobby’s style and vibe.
	•	Turn the photo gallery into a slider with short client quotes layered on each slide.
	•	Use fade-in or slide-in effects on content blocks to give the page a sense of motion and guide attention.
